# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity or
nationality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Give and accept feedback on the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or
block contributors who break these rules. Report problems to
holyphilzy@gmail.com; reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
